# Eorzea Milkers: The Complete Guide

A step-by-step guide to every role, for people who have never played a game like this. Numbers are for version 0.9.99.

## Start here: the words you need and your first five minutes

Eorzea Milkers is an 18+ game that runs inside Final Fantasy XIV through the Dalamud plugin system. It never talks in public chat and never moves your character. Everything happens in its own windows and in your own chat log. Lalafell characters can't play.

### The words used everywhere

- **Dairy**: a place in the game world (a field, a city corner or a house) that an owner has marked as theirs. Most things only count when you're standing inside it.
- **Role**: what you play. There are four: **Cow** (makes milk), **Bull** (makes seed), **Farmer** (looks after cows and bulls and moves milk) and **Owner** (runs the dairy). You can hold more than one at once.
- **Level**: every role has its own level, from 1 to 100. You raise it with **XP** (experience points). Your levels belong to you and follow you to every dairy.
- **Coins**: the money you spend in the Shop.
- **Dairy fund**: a shared money pot for the whole dairy. Only the owner spends it.
- **Dairy tank**: where all the milk and seed the dairy makes ends up. Farmers take milk out of it to deliver.
- **Bottles / vials**: what a cow's milk (bottles) or a bull's seed (vials) fills up while you play. Getting milked empties them.
- **Stall**: a seat the owner registers in the dairy. Sit on it and you get milked automatically.
- **Quota**: your daily target. Reach it, then press Claim in Goals, and you get paid. It is the main way to earn coins.
- **Grade**: how good your milk is: C, B, A, S, S+. A better grade means more pay and more XP.
- **HUD**: the small always-on box on your screen with your bottles, bars and buttons.

### Your first five minutes

1. Type **/milkers** in the chat box and press Enter. The main window opens.
2. The first time, you set up a **save backup**: write down the recovery code it shows you and confirm it. That code gets your whole save back on a new PC. Keep it private.
3. Either join a dairy (someone invites you: the invite shows on the **Dairy** tab, so press Accept) or start your own (Dairy tab > **Start my own dairy**; see the Owner section).
4. Pick your role. You can add **Cow** or **Bull** to yourself any time on Roles or Dairy > Overview. **Farmer** has to be given to you by a farmer or the owner.
5. Type **/milkers hud** if you can't see the HUD. **/milkers guide** opens the in-game guide and **/milkers tutorial** replays the walkthrough.

### The whole game in one paragraph

Cows and bulls slowly fill bottles (or vials) while you're logged in and actually playing. Getting milked, at a stall or by a farmer's hand, empties them into the dairy tank and gives you XP. Each day you have a quota; reach it, claim it, and that's your pay. Farmers keep everyone fed and happy, milk them by hand, and carry milk from the tank to other dairies, which earns the farmer coins and puts coins in the dairy fund. The owner spends the fund on better stalls, better feed and the Velvet pump, which makes everyone's milk come in faster.

## Cows: making milk, getting milked, getting paid

### How milk is made

While you're logged in and **awake** (see the Levels section for asleep), milk slowly fills your bottles. At level 1, with nothing helping, that's about **5 oz an hour**. No milk is made while you're logged out.

You start with one small bottle. When it's full, milk stops until you're milked, so getting milked often matters. Level rewards give you bigger bottles and more of them, up to 4.

Things that make milk come in faster (they add together):

- **Your cow level**: +2% per level, up to level 50 (about double a new cow).
- **Your grade**: B +10%, A +20%, S +35%, S+ +50% milking speed.
- **The four bars** (below) and **Contented** (all four going at once: +10% more).
- **Treats**: +30% for a while.
- **Home pasture**: +10% while you're milked in your own dairy.
- **Velvet pump**: +15% for everyone, when the owner turns it on.
- **Pump drops** (Shop, 60 coins): +25% for 30 minutes.
- **Bred** (by a bull): +5% for 30 minutes.

### The four bars

Each bar is a bonus. When one runs out you just lose that bonus; it never hurts you.

| Bar | How you fill it | Who does it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fed** | Feed from the dairy's feed bin | A **farmer or the owner** feeds you (Herd Care > Feed). There's no Eat button on the network: farmers feed you. |
| **Watered** | Drink | **You** (Barn tab > Drink). Free. |
| **Comfy** | Sit on furniture in your dairy | **You**. After 10 minutes seated you get 30 minutes of Comfy; each further 10 minutes adds 30 more, up to an hour. |
| **Happy** | Treats, grooming | **You** buy treats; a **farmer** grooms you or feeds you baked treats (these are better). |

Each use fills half a bar (30 minutes). Eating, drinking and treats each have a 15-minute wait between uses. Inside a dairy your bars drain half as fast. Bars only count down while you're awake.

### Getting milked: two ways

**1. At a stall.** Sit right on a stall's seat inside the dairy. The machine starts on its own and empties your bottles. Stay seated and it starts again once half a bottle refills. Better stall tiers (Wooden, Padded, Velvet, Gilded) pull milk out faster and collect more of it. A stall a farmer just cleaned gives +10%.

**2. By hand.** A farmer stands within 3 yalms, targets you and presses **Milk** in Herd Care. Milk flows until you're empty or they stop. Hand milking collects more than a basic stall, gives you much more XP, and fixes one grade lost to slipping. If you don't want to be milked without asking, turn off "Farmers can start milking me without asking" on Roles > Cow; then you get an **Allow** pop-up first.

### Your daily quota (this is your pay)

Milking itself doesn't pay coins. Instead, every day a farmer or the owner sets your quota size:

| Size | Milk needed at level 1 | Coins at Grade C |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light | 6 oz | 30 |
| Steady | 12 oz | 60 |
| Heavy | 18 oz | 100 |
| Devoted | 32 oz | 170 |

The milk needed grows 2% per cow level up to 50 (it roughly doubles), because you make milk faster too. When the HUD says **"Quota reached: claim N in Goals"**, open the **Progress > Goals** tab and press **Claim**. You can only claim it once a day.

What makes the payment bigger:

- **Your grade**: C ×1, B ×1.15, A ×1.3, S ×1.45, S+ ×1.6.
- **Wearables** (free level rewards): Show ribbon +5%, Gold-cap bottles +10%, Silk halter +3%, Velvet bell +5%, Velvet champion sash +10%, Heirloom bell +3%, Royal halter +3%.
- **The dairy's level**: +2% per dairy level.

### Daily goals (cow)

Four small goals reset each day at midnight Central time. Each pays **20 coins and 40 XP**, and finishing all four pays **40 more**:

1. Spend 30 minutes in a dairy (awake).
2. Get milked at a stall.
3. Do 3 care tasks (drink, rest, treat and so on).
4. Get hand milked.

The quota is listed with them as a fifth, separate payment.

### Cow XP: where it comes from

- **Making milk**: a little XP for every bit of milk made while you're awake (about 14 XP per oz). A better grade gives more.
- **Being emptied at a stall**: a bonus of 2.75 XP per oz, times the stall's tier bonus.
- **Being hand milked**: 8.25 XP per oz plus 10 for the session, which is about three times a stall.
- **Daily goals**: 40 XP each.
- Care tasks add small amounts too.

### Things to watch

- **Full bottles spoil** after 72 hours (96 with Cooling mist), counting even while you're logged off. Spoiled milk must be dumped for nothing, and your milk tastes "off" (one grade lower) until care and milkings clear it. So get milked before you log off for a long time.
- **Not milked for 3 days**: your grade slips one step, and one more every 3 days after. One milking brings it right back. Going away? A **Dry-off tonic** (150 coins) holds your grade for 7 days.

### What a cow spends coins on

Only consumables: treats (clover 15, salt lick 20, cream clover 35), quality treats (cream biscuit 30, golden alfalfa 80, star clover 200: these push your grade up), Pump drops (60), Udder balm (60: next 3 hand milkings +20%), Second wind tonic (150: tire 2 hours later today), Dry-off tonic (150). Everything else, like bottles, bedding and wearables, is a **free level reward**: it unlocks by itself when you reach its level (Shop > Level rewards). Spare coins are best **donated to the dairy fund**.

## Bulls: seed, breeding and bull XP

A bull works exactly like a cow, except it makes **seed** into **vials** instead of milk into bottles. Everything in the Cows section (the four bars, stalls, hand milking, grades, spoiling, the Shop) applies to bulls too. This section only covers what's different.

### How seed is made

- Vials hold **a tenth** of what a bottle holds.
- Seed comes in at **15% of milk's speed at Grade C**, rising to 20% at S+. So a bull makes a lot less by volume than a cow; that's normal.
- All the same speed bonuses help: bull level (+2% a level up to 50), bars, treats, home pasture, the Velvet pump and Pump drops.
- Getting milked (stall or hand) empties your vials into the dairy's **seed tank**.

### The bull's daily quota

A bull's quota is in seed: 0.17 oz of seed for every 1 oz a cow would need. It pays the same coins as a cow's.

| Size | Seed needed at level 1 | Coins at Grade C |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light | about 1 oz | 30 |
| Steady | about 2 oz | 60 |
| Heavy | about 3 oz | 100 |
| Devoted | about 5.4 oz | 170 |

The same grade multiplier applies (C ×1 up to S+ ×1.6), plus the dairy's level, plus bull wearables: Nose ring +3%, Brass horn caps +5% and Champion bull sash +10%. If you're both a cow and a bull, your quota is in milk.

### Breeding

Breeding works with the **Corruption** plugin. When a bull from your dairy finishes in a scene with a cow, she is **bred**: +5% milk for 30 minutes (45 with the Champion's ribbon). A cow can be bred again 20 minutes later.

- A bull from **another** dairy gets a pop-up on the cow's side; it only counts if she presses **Allow**.
- If both partners are cow *and* bull, the one doing the breeding targets the other and presses **Breed** on the HUD (or right-click > Eorzea Milkers > Breed). Only that counts.
- A bull can breed as often as they like, but after **20 breedings a day** their seed output drops. The Stud book raises that to 25 and the Legendary stud to 30.
- **Breeding stalls**: the owner can switch a stall to breeding. A cow sits in it for 5 minutes and is bred (no pregnancy; that only comes from Corruption). Each use takes 3 oz from the dairy's seed tank. Farmers load these stalls.
- **Pregnancy** comes from Corruption and adds milk by trimester until the birth.

### Bull XP: where it comes from

- **Making seed**: XP as seed comes in, but bulls get 40% of what the same amount of milk would give a cow, because they also level from breeding.
- **Being emptied** at a stall or by hand: the same kind of bonus as a cow.
- **Each breeding: 40 bull XP** at full output (+25% with Prize stud, +25% for 30 minutes with a Bull's salt lick).
- **Being fed by a farmer: 20 bull XP** each time.
- **Daily goals**: 40 XP each (a bull who isn't also a cow has "Give 1 oz of seed" instead of the 10 oz milk goal).

### Bull-only Shop items

Iron oats (30 coins: fills Watered), Bull's salt lick (35: Happy, +25% bull XP for 30 minutes), Seed tea (40: next seed milking +25%), Stud draught (120: next breeding gives 50% more bred bonus, lasting 15 minutes longer). Twin blend (60) and the Velvet & brass bonbon (50) work for both cows and bulls.

### Bull level rewards (free)

| Level | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Nose ring: +3% seed quota pay |
| 10 | Stud book: 25 full breedings a day |
| 20 | Brass horn caps: +5% seed quota pay |
| 25 | Prize stud: +25% bull XP from breeding |
| 35 | Breeding pen: each breeding in your dairy gives the dairy XP |
| 50 | Herd sire: seed fills vials 15% faster |
| 75 | Champion bull sash: +10% seed quota pay, breeding output never under 60% |
| 100 | Legendary stud: 30 full breedings a day |

## Farmers: care, hand milking, the kitchen and deliveries

The farmer is the dairy's worker. They keep cows and bulls fed and happy, milk them by hand, and carry milk out of the tank to other dairies. Farmers are the main way coins come **into** the dairy fund. You can't give yourself Farmer: a farmer or the owner has to give it to you.

Your main tool is **Herd Care**. Open it from the HUD button or Roles > Farmer. It lists every cow and bull in the dairy, how close they are and how full.

### Chores (all done inside the dairy)

| Chore | How | Farmer XP | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Feed from the feed bin** | Herd Care > pick a cow or bull > Feed > pick a bin feed. Fills their **Fed** bar. Free and unlimited. | 10 | Once per cow every 15 min |
| **Feed a baked treat** | Herd Care > Feed > pick a treat from the dairy shelf. Gives Happy and a milk boost (bulls get 20 bull XP). | 10 | Once per cow every 15 min |
| **Groom** | Stand next to them, press Groom. +30 minutes of Happy. | 20 | Once per cow every 15 min |
| **Clean a stall** | Press Clean on a stall nobody is sitting on. Cows milked there get +10% speed and XP for an hour. | 25 | Again when half an hour is left |
| **Ring the feeding bell** | Herd Care > Bell. Every cow and bull in the dairy fills one bar right up and gets a 20-minute boost. | 10 | Once an hour per dairy |
| **Load a breeding stall** | Loads one use of a breeding stall from the seed tank. | 8 | Up to 5 loaded |

The better the dairy's feed bin (the owner buys upgrades), the bigger the Fed bonus you give: Wheat grain +4%, Sweet grain +7%, Honey oats +9%, Velvet blend +12%, Golden mash +15%.

### Hand milking

1. Stand within 3 yalms of the cow or bull and target them.
2. In Herd Care press **Milk**. Your plugin does your milking emote or pose for you.
3. Milk flows until they're empty, or until you stop, step away or change target.

You're paid a **share** of what that milk is worth: 25% (30% from farmer level 45; 10% more for a cow with the Champion's ribbon), with no daily limit. Each payment also gives farmer XP (an eighth of the coins, at least 5). Udder balm on the cow gives 20% more milk.

### The kitchen: baking treats

Open **Roles > Farmer > Kitchen**. First fill your jugs from the dairy tank at the top (1 oz a second, twice as fast with **Churn oil**, 40 coins; you have to stay in the dairy while it fills). Then bake:

| Recipe | Needs | Makes | Farmer level | XP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Butter | 16 oz milk | 1 butter | 1 | 12 |
| Cheese | 16 oz milk | 2 cheese | 1 | 10 |
| Butter cookies | 1 butter + 8 oz | 3 cookies | 3 | 15 |
| Flour | 2 wheat grain (Shop) | 1 flour | 6 | 6 |
| Cream buns | 1 flour + 1 butter + 12 oz | 2 buns | 10 | 25 |
| Honey cream cake | 1 flour + 2 butter + 16 oz | 2 cakes | 35 | 40 |
| Velvet tart | 2 flour + 2 butter + 24 oz | 2 tarts | 70 | 60 |

Baked treats go on the **dairy shelf**. Members buy them (you get 80% of the price and the fund gets the rest), or you feed them to cows for free from Herd Care.

### Deliveries: where farmer coins come from

Your **farmer tank** is how much milk you can carry: a 32 oz pail at first. Buy bigger tanks in **Shop > Farmer tank** (200, 400, 900, 1,600, 2,500, 4,000 and 7,500 coins, unlocking at farmer levels 2, 5, 10, 16, 22, 30 and 40, up to 400 oz).

- **Drop-offs** (Creamery > Deliveries): walk into any other dairy and leave milk from your jugs in their tank, no order needed. The Grand Creamery pays you 1.5 coins an oz, your own **dairy fund gets 2 coins per oz**, and you get 2.5 farmer XP an oz.
- **Other dairies' buy orders** (Creamery > Deliveries): the milk goes into their tank and the coins they offered go to you.
- **Daily delivery limit**: 30 oz a day to other dairies at farmer level 1, plus 5 oz a level (275 at level 50, 525 at level 100). Drop-offs and buy orders share it. You can also make only **10 deliveries a day**, and drop off at each dairy **once a day** (until the daily reset).
- **The weekly contract**: take jugs to the Grand Creamery (Dairy > Contract shows where) and ship. Each shipment gives you farmer XP (half the oz) and the dairy XP and fund coins.

Filling jugs also gives farmer XP (a quarter of the oz).

### The farmer's daily quota

A farmer's quota counts **milk you deliver** (drop-offs, buy orders and the contract), and the owner sets its size separately from any cow quota you have: 3 times a cow's amount, paying 30% more and not affected by grade.

| Size | Delivered at level 1 | Coins |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light | 18 oz | 39 |
| Steady | 36 oz | 78 |
| Heavy | 54 oz | 130 |
| Devoted | 96 oz | 221 |

### Farmer daily goals

Each pays 20 coins and 40 farmer XP (+40 coins for all four): **Groom 2 cows**, **Clean a stall**, **Deliver to another dairy or ship to the contract**, **Hand milk a cow until they're empty**. If you're also a cow you get both sets.

### Farmer XP summary

Groom 20 · Clean 25 · Feed 10 · Bell 10 · Load breeding stall 8 · Baking 6–60 · Filling jugs ¼ per oz · Hand milking pay ⅛ of coins (min 5) · Contract ½ per oz · Drop-offs 2.5 per oz · Goals 40 each.

## Owners: running the dairy

The owner builds the dairy, invites people, sets quotas and spends the dairy fund. **An owner has no role to start with.** You don't have to be a cow, bull or farmer, but you can add any of them and play them normally. The owner can also do every farmer job (feed, bell, fill jugs, deliver) without being a farmer.

### Setting up a dairy, step by step

1. Walk to where you want it: a field, a quiet city corner, or a room in a house.
2. Open **/milkers > Dairy tab**. Type a name, choose public or private, and whether your name shows.
3. Stand in the middle and press **Start marking here**. A box appears on the ground (pink sides, blue front). Use Width, Length and Turn until it covers your dairy. Height is how far up or down still counts.
4. Press **Register my dairy**. Boxes can't overlap another dairy in the same zone and world.
5. **Stalls**: sit on a seat inside the box and press **Add** on Dairy > Stalls. The first stall is free.
6. **Invite people**: right-click a player > Eorzea Milkers > Invite as cow, bull or farmer. Or target them and use the Members panel.

You can move or resize the box at any time (Dairy > Listing > Move or resize), and add a **private place**, such as your house, that only members count as being in.

### Managing members and quotas

Open **Manage members** from Dairy > Overview. From there you can give or remove roles, remove people, and set each member's **Daily quota** (Light, Steady, Heavy or Devoted). Farmers can set quotas too. A bigger quota needs more milk but pays more, so match it to how much each person plays:

- **Light**: plays under an hour a day.
- **Steady**: about 1–2 hours.
- **Heavy**: about 2–4 hours.
- **Devoted**: plays most of the day.

Only the owner takes roles away.

### The dairy fund: where it comes from

- **Donations** from members.
- **Drop-offs**: 2 coins per oz your farmers leave at other dairies.
- **The weekly contract** reward.
- **Shelf sales**: 20% of every baked treat sold.

### What to spend the fund on

**Stalls.** The first is free; each new one costs 500 × (stalls you already have)²: 500, 2,000, 4,500, 8,000, 12,500… (up to 16).

**Stall tiers** (each stall separately):

| Tier | Cost | Needs dairy level | Speed | Milk collected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wooden | free | 1 | 0.2 oz/s | ×1.0 |
| Padded | 300 | 1 | 0.3 oz/s | ×1.1 |
| Velvet | 1,000 | 5 | 0.4 oz/s | ×1.2 |
| Gilded | 2,500 | 12 | 0.6 oz/s | ×1.35 |

**The feed bin** (Dairy > Feed & pump). Farmers feed from it for free, as much as they like. Better feed gives a bigger Fed bonus to everyone:

| Feed | Fed bonus | Needs dairy level | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wheat grain | +4% | 1 | free |
| Sweet grain | +7% | 3 | 2,000 |
| Honey oats | +9% | 6 | 6,000 |
| Velvet blend | +12% | 10 | 15,000 |
| Golden mash | +15% | 15 | 35,000 |

**The Velvet pump** (Dairy > Feed & pump, from dairy level 5): 2,500 coins turns on +15% milk and seed for everyone in the dairy for 2 hours. Best used when lots of people are on.

**Buy orders** (Creamery > Deliveries): ask other dairies for milk. The coins are held until someone fills the order or you cancel.

**Shelf prices**: set what members pay for each baked treat, within a range.

### Dairy level

The dairy has its own level, **1 to 20**. Members earn dairy XP by milking and caring inside it (up to 300 each per day), plus filled buy orders and contracts. Each level needs 250 more than the last (400, then 650, then 900…): about 3,100 XP total for level 5, 12,600 for level 10 and 18,150 for level 12.

Each level gives **+2% quota pay** to everyone, unlocks better stall tiers (Velvet at 5, Gilded at 12) and feed (levels 3, 6, 10, 15), the pump at 5, and ranks the dairy higher on the Dairies list.

### The weekly contract

Every Monday at 00:00 UTC (the weekly contract still runs on UTC) the Grand Creamery gives the dairy a contract: **600 oz at dairy level 1, plus 150 oz a level** (up to 3,500). Any milk counts. Farmers or the owner fill jugs and ship them at the Grand Creamery. When it's full, the dairy gets 1.5 dairy XP and 4 fund coins per oz of the contract (600 oz = 900 XP and 2,400 coins). If it isn't full by Monday you still get half of the share you delivered.

**Weekend milk contest** (Saturday and Sunday, UTC): milk pumped into the tank also fills the contract, so it counts twice.

## Coins: every way in and every way out

There is **no daily coin cap**. The only brake is getting **tired** after 12 hours of making milk in a day, which halves your speed (and so halves how fast you reach your quota).

### Ways to earn coins

| Source | Who | How much |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily quota** | Cow, bull, farmer | 30 / 60 / 100 / 170 (farmer 39 / 78 / 130 / 221), times grade, wearables and dairy level |
| **Daily goals** | Everyone with a role | 20 each, +40 for all four (60 × 2 sets if you're cow and farmer) |
| **Hand milking share** | Farmer | 25–30% of the milk's value, no daily limit |
| **Drop-offs at other dairies** | Farmer, owner | 1.5 per oz, and 2/oz to your dairy fund (daily limit by farmer level) |
| **Other dairies' buy orders** | Farmer, owner | Whatever the buyer offered |
| **Shelf sales** | Farmer (baker) | 80% of the price |
| **Selling back** | Everyone | Pantry items at half price |

Milking itself, at a stall or by hand, **does not pay the cow or bull**. Their pay is the quota.

### Ways to spend coins

- **Consumables** (Shop > Feed & treats): treats, quality treats, tonics, Pump drops, Udder balm, Churn oil.
- **Farmer tanks** (Shop > Farmer tank): the only upgrade still bought with coins.
- **Baked treats** from the dairy shelf.
- **Donating** to the dairy fund (Dairy tab). This is the best use of spare coins: it pays for stalls, the feed bin and the pump, which help everyone.

### Level rewards are free

Bottles, extra bottles, bedding, troughs, wearables and the rest unlock **on their own** when you reach their level (Shop > Level rewards shows what's next). Upgrades bought before this change weren't refunded.

## Levels, grades, goals, awards, and being tired or asleep

### Levels

Cow, bull and farmer each have their own level, from 1 to 100. Gaining or losing a role never loses its progress. Each level takes more XP than the last:

- Level 2 needs **80 XP**.
- Around level 10, about **300 XP** per level.
- Around level 50, about **4,700 XP** per level.
- In the 90s, **13,000–16,000 XP** per level.

The first ten levels go by in an evening or two; the 90s take days each. Every 25 levels you get a new title. Cow and bull levels make milk or seed 2% faster per level (up to 50). Farmer level unlocks bigger tanks, new recipes and a bigger daily delivery limit.

### Grades (milk and seed quality)

Everyone starts at **Grade C**. You build **quality points** and move up when you pass each mark:

| Grade | Quality needed | Quota pay | Milking speed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C | 0 | ×1 | normal |
| B | 150 | ×1.15 | +10% |
| A | 800 | ×1.3 | +20% |
| S | 2,600 | ×1.45 | +35% |
| S+ | 6,000 | ×1.6 | +50% |

Where quality points come from: **5 per oz** milked at a stall, **7.5 per oz** milked by hand, **10** per creamery delivery, and quality treats: cream biscuit +20, golden alfalfa +50, star clover +100 (one quality treat an hour).

Your grade **slips one step** if you go 3 days without being milked (and another every 3 days after). One milking brings it back; a Dry-off tonic pauses it for 7 days. Spoiled milk makes your milk "off" for a while (one grade lower).

### Daily goals

They reset every day. Each pays 20 coins and 40 XP, and all four pay 40 more. Cows and bulls have one set, farmers another; if you're both you get both. Your quota appears with them and is claimed there (Progress > Goals > **Claim**).

### Awards (badges, one time)

First pail (first milking) · Personal touch (first hand milking) · Twenty ounces · Two hundred ounces · Milk river (1,000 oz) · Rich and creamy (Grade A) · Cream of the crop (Grade S) · Pure velvet (Grade S+) · Open for business (first delivery) · Regular supplier (25 deliveries) · Well kept (50 care tasks) · Routine (7 daily goals) · Gilded (a Gilded stall) · Well off (earn 5,000 coins).

### Awake, dozing and asleep

Milk is only for people who are actually playing. Real **keyboard, mouse or controller input** in the game keeps you awake, as does clicking in Eorzea Milkers.

| State | When | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awake | You did something recently | Everything works normally |
| **Dozing** | 15 min with no input (30 if seated in your dairy) | Milk at half speed; stalls don't start a new milking |
| **Asleep** | 30 min with no input (45 if seated in your dairy) | No milk, no XP until you do anything |

Bars and treat timers pause while you're dozing, asleep or logged out, so you don't lose them.

### Tired

After **12 hours of making milk in one day** you're tired: milk (and seed) comes in at half speed until the daily reset (midnight Central time). Steady producer (level 18) makes it 14 hours, Night owl (level 62) adds 2 more, and a Second wind tonic adds 2 hours for one day.

### A day in each role, at a glance

- **Cow / bull**: log in → walk into your dairy → drink, sit somewhere comfy, have a treat → sit on a stall (or ask a farmer to hand milk you) → keep playing until the HUD says Quota reached → Goals > Claim.
- **Farmer**: log in → ring the bell → feed everyone from the feed bin → groom two cows → clean a stall → hand milk someone empty → fill jugs → drop milk off at another dairy or ship to the contract → claim goals and your quota → bake treats for the shelf.
- **Owner**: check the fund → set quotas (and farmer quotas) for new members → buy the next stall tier or feed when you can afford it → turn on the Velvet pump when lots of people are on → keep the weekly contract moving.

### Something's wrong

| Problem | Why, and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| No milk is coming in | Your bottles are full (get milked), you're asleep (do anything), you're tired (wait for the daily reset), or you're logged out. |
| The stall won't start | Sit **on** the seat itself, not next to it, inside the dairy. If you're dozing, move or click something. |
| I can't feed myself | Correct: in a networked dairy a farmer or the owner feeds you from the feed bin. You drink, rest and buy treats yourself. |
| I hit my quota but got no coins | Open Progress > Goals and press **Claim**. |
| My grade went down | You weren't milked for 3 days, or your milk is "off" after spoiling. Get milked; care tasks clear the off taste. |
| My milk spoiled | Full bottles spoil after 72 hours. Dump it from the HUD, and get milked before long breaks. |
| Hand milking does nothing | The farmer must be within 3 yalms, targeting you, and use the Milk button. If you turned off auto-milking, press Allow. |
| I'm not falling asleep while AFK | Copy the **Awake:** line from Settings > Support and send it in on the Discord. |
| Nothing happens in my dairy | Check the network is on (Settings). Playing offline runs the game on its own, with no dairy. |
| New PC or reinstall | Use your recovery code (Settings or /milkers restore), or get a restore key from #restore-save in the Discord. |

Questions, finding a dairy and update news: the Discord (18+), linked from the plugin.
